## 🟧 About

This is my personal developer portfolio, built to present my **technical journey and projects in one place**.

The portfolio reflects my transition from **Web Development towards DevOps & Cloud**, while also showcasing the web technologies and development experience I have built along the way.

The website focuses on a **clean, modern and interactive experience** rather than simply listing information.

---

## ✦ What You'll Find

```text
01  About        →  My background & journey
02  Skills       →  Technologies I'm working with
03  Projects     →  Hands-on work I've built
04  Contact      →  Ways to connect with me
```

The site includes **responsive design, light/dark themes, smooth animations and interactive UI elements** to keep the experience simple and engaging.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

`React` `JavaScript` `Tailwind CSS` `GSAP` `HTML` `CSS`

---

## 🟧 Design Philosophy

> **Simple. Interactive. Developer-focused.**

The design is intentionally minimal, with subtle motion, a focused colour system and responsive layouts designed to keep attention on the content rather than unnecessary visual elements.

---

## 🤖 Development Note

AI tools were used as a **development assistant** during the creation of this portfolio for brainstorming, debugging, learning and refining parts of the implementation. The final design and implementation were customised and reviewed by me.
